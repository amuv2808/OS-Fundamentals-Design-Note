Overall Conclusion:
Operating systems play an essential role in modern computing. They manage processes, allocate memory, organize files, control storage devices, and maintain system security.
Through this project, the fundamental concepts of operating systems and their practical importance have been explored.
Understanding these concepts provides a foundation for advanced computer science subjects such as system programming, computer architecture, cloud computing, and cybersecurity.
